## Sequencing logic

- **Weeks 1–4:** all Tier-1/Tier-2 — get ranking traffic fast and prove the engine.
- **Weeks 5–9:** keep harvesting quick wins while adding mid-difficulty comparisons.
- **Weeks 10–12:** publish the big Tier-3 "Best X" anchor pages, now that you have internal-link equity from 20+ supporting posts.

## Internal linking (do this every post)
Each **Review** links up to its **Comparison** and its **Roundup**. Example cluster:
`Is Claude Pro Worth It` → `Claude vs ChatGPT` → `Best AI Chatbot`. This topic-cluster structure is the #1 way a young site outranks bigger ones.

## Newsletter
The **Sunday roundup is the subscription promise.** Every review/comparison ends with: *"We test every new AI tool — get the verdict in your inbox each Sunday."* Capture email on-page; the roundup is what keeps them.
