# Low-Ticket Product Brief — "The SMB AI Marketing Automation Starter Kit"

*The entry rung of the value ladder. Its job at zero audience is NOT revenue — it's to (a) qualify
buyers, and (b) prove you can do the done-for-you work, so the $29 buyer self-selects into a
"just set it up for me" booked call. Every asset inside is a smaller version of the consulting deliverable.*

---

## The offer in one line

**The exact AI marketing automations I install for small businesses — packaged so you can set up the
basics yourself in a weekend, or hand it to me to do for you.**

- **Price:** $29 (launch at **$19** for the first 2 weeks / first 50 buyers, then $29).
- **Format:** Gumroad digital download — a PDF playbook + copy-paste templates + importable automation files.
- **Audience:** small-business owners and solo/in-house marketers who know AI could save them hours but
  don't know what to automate first.

## Why this product and not a prompt pack

A "500 ChatGPT prompts" pack is undifferentiated, cheapens the brand, and attracts non-buyers. This kit is
the **artifact of a real service** — it demonstrates competence, which is what turns a reader into a
consulting lead. The buyer who pays $29 and sees the quality is the warmest possible prospect for
done-for-you AI setup.

---

## What's inside (client-grade, not filler)

### 1. Five plug-and-play marketing automations
Each with a step-by-step setup guide (screenshots) for **Make / Zapier / n8n**, plus an importable
template where possible:

1. **Lead → instant personalised follow-up.** Form or Calendly booking → AI-drafted, personalised reply →
   logged to a CRM/Google Sheet. (Speed-to-lead is the highest-ROI automation for SMBs.)
2. **Inbox triage + AI draft replies.** Incoming email → categorised → a ready-to-send draft waiting in Gmail.
3. **Content repurposer.** One blog post → 5 social posts + a newsletter blurb. (This is literally the
   workflow behind Hevesi Research — eat your own dog food.)
4. **Review / testimonial engine.** Post-purchase trigger → AI-personalised review request → thank-you +
   reply draft when one lands.
5. **Plain-English weekly marketing report.** GA4 + Search Console → an AI summary of "what changed and what
   to do about it," emailed every Monday.

### 2. The marketing prompt library (50+, organised by job)
Ad copy · email sequences · SEO content briefs · social captions · landing-page CRO · customer research.
Not a dump — grouped by the task you're trying to finish, with fill-in-the-blank variables.

### 3. Setup SOPs + guardrails
Connecting tools and API keys · the **"what to automate first" decision tree** · a one-page **"is this
worth automating?" ROI calculator** · and the non-negotiable guardrails (never paste client PII, turn off
training-on-your-data, human-in-the-loop on anything customer-facing).

### 4. The tool stack
Exactly which AI tools to pay for to run all of the above — linking back to the Hevesi Research reviews
(internal monetisation loop + reinforces the "I test these" authority).

## Funnel placement (how it ladders)

1. **Free lead magnet** — give away automation #1 (Lead → instant follow-up) as a PDF to grow the email list.
2. **$29 kit** — the full set. Pitched in the newsletter, in post CTAs, and on the homepage.
3. **Done-for-you AI setup for SMBs** — upsell *inside* the kit and on the Gumroad receipt/thank-you page:
   *"Don't want to build it yourself? I'll install the whole stack in your business — book a call."* → Calendly.

This is the bridge that makes the product worth building: it pays for itself as a **sales asset for the
consulting**, regardless of unit sales.
