# Keyword Research — davidhevesi.com

*Source: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, US (google), pulled 2026-06-28. Volume = monthly US searches. KD = Keyword Difficulty (0–100, lower = easier to rank). TP = Traffic Potential (clicks the top page actually gets). Global = worldwide monthly volume.*

## How to read this

- **KD 0–10 = quick win.** A newer site can realistically rank these within weeks–months. Prioritise.
- **KD 11–30 = mid-term.** Winnable with a strong post + a few links. Your bread and butter.
- **KD 31+ = long game.** Write them for authority and future payoff, but don't expect fast ranking.
- **Volume isn't everything.** A KD-3 keyword at 6,100 searches beats a KD-54 keyword at 34,000 for a young site — you can actually win the first one.

## Strategic takeaways

1. **Front-load Tier 1 "is it worth it" reviews.** They're the fastest path to your first ranking traffic and they're literally your homepage's existing voice.
2. **Sprinkle in the low-KD comparisons** (cursor vs copilot KD 6, chatgpt vs gemini KD 15) — best traffic-per-effort on the list.
3. **Publish Tier 3 roundups steadily but patiently.** Link to them from every relevant review/comparison so they accrue authority.
4. **Every review should internal-link to its comparison and roundup** (e.g. "Is Claude Pro Worth It" → "Claude vs ChatGPT" → "Best AI Chatbot"). This topic-cluster structure is how small sites punch above their weight.
